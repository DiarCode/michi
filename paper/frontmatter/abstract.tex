\chapter{Abstract}
\begin{SingleSpace}

Accurate real-time prediction of passenger flows in urban bus networks is essential for operational planning, crowd management, and strategic capacity allocation. Existing approaches often fail to jointly capture the complex spatial dependencies between stations, the multi-timescale temporal dynamics of ridership, and the uncertainty inherent in count-based demand data. We present DTS-GSSF (Dual-Timescale Graph Gated Forecasting), a hybrid deep learning architecture designed for multi-horizon passenger flow prediction on the Astana bus network. The model integrates a Gated Recurrent Encoder for temporal feature extraction, adaptive graph propagation for spatial diffusion across the transit network, and multi-head temporal attention for capturing long-range dependencies. LoRA-based adaptation enables efficient fine-tuning for route-level specialization. We evaluate DTS-GSSF on a synthetic dataset of 1.3 million ridership records across 374 bus stations over 12 months, generated from OpenStreetMap topology and realistic demand simulation. On the held-out test set, DTS-GSSF achieves an R-squared of $0.978 \pm 0.001$ (3 seeds) and a mean absolute error of $11.65 \pm 0.14$ passengers per station-hour across all 42 series (28 stations + 14 hierarchical aggregates), outperforming ten baselines including Historical Average, LSTM, GRU, TCN, STGCN, Graph WaveNet, and AGCRN. Ablation studies confirm that graph propagation contributes $\Delta R^2 = +0.254$ and temporal attention contributes $\Delta R^2 = +0.066$. Integrated Gradients analysis reveals weather features (temperature, precipitation) as the strongest predictors. The model converges within 30 epochs on consumer-grade hardware and maintains $R^2 > 0.95$ under 30\% station dropout and moderate input noise, demonstrating practical applicability for real-time transit intelligence platforms.

\vspace{5mm}
\noindent{\small{\bf Keywords:} passenger flow prediction, graph neural networks, gated recurrent models, spatiotemporal forecasting, urban transit, deep learning, Astana bus network}

\end{SingleSpace}
