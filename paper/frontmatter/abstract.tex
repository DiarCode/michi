\chapter{Abstract}
\begin{SingleSpace}

Accurate real-time forecasting of passenger boarding demand is a prerequisite for dynamic vehicle dispatching, proactive crowd management, and strategic capacity allocation in urban bus networks, yet existing approaches rarely capture jointly the complex spatial dependencies between stations, the multi-timescale temporal dynamics of ridership, and the overdispersed uncertainty inherent in count data. This thesis introduces DTS-GSSF (Dual-Timescale Graph Gated Forecasting), a hybrid deep learning architecture that combines a single-gate recurrent encoder for local temporal resolution, multi-head self-attention for long-range dependencies, and a dual graph propagation mechanism that fuses a physical route adjacency with a learnable adaptive matrix, while producing a Negative Binomial distributional output for calibrated uncertainty quantification. Evaluation is conducted on two complementary benchmarks: a synthetic Astana bus network (1.3~million station-hour records across 374 stations over 12~months, derived from OpenStreetMap topology and a heuristic demand simulator) and a publicly available LACMTA open dataset (180~stops, 9~routes, 62{,}304 windows). On the synthetic Astana test set, DTS-GSSF attains an $R^2 = 0.978 \pm 0.001$ and MAE $= 11.65 \pm 0.14$ passengers per station-hour on the full 42-series hierarchical evaluation; on bottom-level stations the model reaches $R^2 = 0.692$, comparable to strong neural baselines. On the real-world LACMTA benchmark, DTS-GSSF achieves $R^2 = 0.862$ and MAE = 7.1, the best result among all evaluated methods, providing a more realistic indicator of operational performance. Ablation studies confirm that graph propagation contributes $\Delta R^2 = +0.254$ and temporal attention $\Delta R^2 = +0.066$. The contribution of the thesis is a deployable, edge-ready architecture that demonstrates a coherent integration of gated recurrence, adaptive graph reasoning, multi-head attention, and distributional output, with sub-5~ms inference latency and a 542K-parameter footprint suitable for real-time transit intelligence platforms.

\vspace{5mm}
\noindent{\small{\bf Keywords:} passenger flow prediction, graph neural networks, gated recurrent models, spatiotemporal forecasting, urban transit, deep learning, Astana bus network}

\end{SingleSpace}
